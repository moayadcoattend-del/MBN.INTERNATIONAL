About MBN International

Built On Trust, Integrity & Full Regulatory Compliance

Headquartered in Riyadh, MBN International is a premier general construction contractor and workforce mobilization powerhouse supporting the rapid infrastructural transformation of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

We bridge critical capacity gaps for top-tier contracting companies through end-to-end site preparation, civil subcontracting, and high-caliber human capital. All our manpower operations comply rigorously with Qiwa, Muqeem, and the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) regulations.
