About MBN International

Pioneering Civil Infrastructure & Workforce Solutions Across the Kingdom

Established to meet the rigorous demands of Saudi Arabia's rapid industrial expansion under Vision 2030, MBN International has grown from an agile engineering subcontractor to one of the region's most reputable multi-disciplinary general contracting and manpower solutions powerhouses.

We bridge the gap between complex engineering requirements and on-ground workforce mobilization. With strategic hubs in Riyadh, Dammam, and Jeddah, we maintain a registered talent bank exceeding thousands of verified specialists ready for immediate deployment.

Full Compliance

100% Qiwa, GOSI, Muqeem, and wage-protection statutory adherence.

Rapid Mobilization

Pre-screened cohorts dispatched within 48 to 72 hours country-wide.

End-to-End Care
